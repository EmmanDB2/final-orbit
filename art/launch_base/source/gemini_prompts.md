# Launch base: Gemini prompts

Three buildings, one image each. For every prompt, attach two images from this folder:

1. `base_reference.png`: the astronaut, the colors, the lander, and a plan of the whole base at true scale.
2. That building's template: `assembly_template.png`, `tower_template.png`, or `mission_control_template.png`.

Then paste the prompt. Save the results here as `assembly.png`, `tower.png`, and `mission_control.png`.

If a result comes back with the outside and the cutaway not matching, ask for them as two images: first "Draw only the OUTSIDE", then "Draw only the CUTAWAY of the same building", attaching the first result to the second.

---

## 1. Vehicle assembly building

Create a pixel-art building for a 2D side-view sci-fi game: the vehicle assembly building of a 1970s NASA-style rocket launch base. Clean off-white wall panels, gray steel, orange and blue agency stripes, gold foil accents, and chunky, readable shapes.

The second attached image is the template to fill. It has two panels side by side: OUTSIDE and CUTAWAY. Both show the same building at the same size, standing on the same white ground line.

OUTSIDE: a tall, boxy hangar with ribbed off-white wall panels.

- A huge vehicle door at the right end, where the tall dashed box is, running nearly the full height, with orange-and-white warning stripes on its frame.
- A normal-sized door at the lower left, where the small dashed box is.
- A few small square windows high up, and a wide orange and blue stripe across the wall, with no lettering.
- Warning lights along the roof edge, and a small crane housing on the roof.

CUTAWAY: the same building with its front wall removed, so the inside shows. The outline, roof, side walls, and floor stay exactly as in OUTSIDE, and only the front wall is gone.

- One tall bay. On the floor where the box marked SHIP is, a low ship assembly cradle with clamps and hoses. Leave the space above the cradle empty: no rocket or ship.
- An overhead gantry crane rail under the roof, spanning the bay, with a hook hanging from a trolley.
- On the left, two catwalks with railings at the heights of the dashed lines, joined to the floor by a ladder where the box marked LADDER is.
- A small control booth with a console on the upper catwalk.
- Tool racks and parts crates along the back wall on the floor.
- The vehicle door, seen from inside, is closed.
- Floors and catwalks are solid, flat, continuous slabs, so a person could walk along them.

Size: the off-white box marked PILOT in the template is one astronaut standing, 1.85 m tall. Draw an astronaut there, in the style of the attached one, standing on the ground line, as a size reference. The small door is a little taller than the astronaut.

Pixel-art rules:

- Seen exactly from the side, straight on, like an architectural elevation: no perspective, and no top or side faces showing.
- True pixel art on a crisp, uniform pixel grid: every pixel is a clean square block of the same size.
- No anti-aliasing, blur, gradients, glow, soft shadows, or noise. Light comes from the upper left.
- Use only these colors: #0E0F12, #23262B, #3D4148, #6B7079, #A9ADB3, #E8E4DA, #D9541E, #F28A3A, #7A2E12, #C8302C, #1B2E4A, #2F5D8C, #6FA3C8, #5A3E12, #9C6B1E, #D4A23A, #F2D27A, #2A4A55, #8FC7CF, and for small indicator lights only #6BCB5A and #F2B33A.
- Every shape has a 1-pixel near-black outline (#0E0F12).
- The background is flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. Draw no ground below the line, and no sky, shadows on the ground, text, labels, numbers, or borders.
- The template's white boxes, dashed lines, and labels are guides only. Follow them, but don't draw them.
