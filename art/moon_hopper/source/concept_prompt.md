# Moon Hopper concept

Made 2026-11-14 with the Antigravity CLI, in a scratch folder: `agy -p "<prompt>" --sandbox`, about
5 minutes. Manny approved it the same day as the look to build from in the Asset Editor
(`docs/briefs/moon_hopper_in_asset_editor.md`). It's a concept: not on the grid, not in the palette.

Prompt:

> Make a pixel art concept image of a small one-seat moon hopper spacecraft for a 2D side-view space
> game. Style: NASApunk, 1960s-70s space agency hardware, Apollo-era feel but an original design. Seen
> exactly from the side, nose up, mirror-symmetric left to right. Shape: squat and wide, the body about
> one and a half times as wide as it is tall, not counting the legs. A round glass bubble cockpit for
> one pilot sits in the middle on top, with a small round docking hatch ring on the very top of the
> bubble. At both top shoulders, an RCS thruster cluster on a short outrigger. Two round fuel tanks
> wrapped in gold foil hang low on the left and right sides. One small engine bell at the bottom
> center. Two landing legs splayed wide, with broad round foot pads, the feet lower than the engine
> bell. Colors: white and light gray hull, dark gray structure, agency orange and navy blue stripes,
> gold foil tanks, teal-tinted glass. Flat colors only, no gradients, no painted shading or highlights,
> no shadows, chunky clean pixels, a 1-pixel near-black outline around everything. Plain flat dark gray
> background, nothing else in the picture, no text, no stars. Save it here as hopper_concept.png
